# Greed Ranks

Replaces greed tiers as the main form of progression, with the theme being you’re a vault hunter ranking up in their skill instead of some arbitrary tier system. Ranks still need reputation to increase, though reputation is now gained in multiple different ways. You will also need regular experience levels, and enough God Levels to rank up as well.

## Reputation Gain

Reputation is now gained through persistent milestone goals instead of the old greed quests system. Milestones have global progress across all vaults, and are designed to encompass the general vault activities one would normally do – mining vault ores, killing mobs, looting chests, and so on. These milestones have various tiers, each harder than the last, and granting more reputation. The idea is that players are naturally encouraged to do a wide variety of activities to fulfill milestones over the course of their gameplay instead of target farming ranks, and that earlier ranks come more easily while later ranks take a lot more time grinding out all of the different achievements.

In addition, challenges aren’t removed in this version, but instead replaced with a challenges screen where completing challenge crystals completes challenge milestones, which in turn reward rep. Challenge tiers are also gated by rank, and what challenges are available are also gated to prevent *only* using challenges to rank up. Challenges in general give less rep% than before, to limit their usefulness but still have them around. 

The full panel of milestones with rep and goal numbers can be found on the spreadsheet. 

## Greed Shop

The greed shop stays mostly the same, but has the weight of what it is selling changed slightly, as well as prices, and is now rerolled with Greedy Tickets instead of reputation, since reputation is much more finite in this version. The places Greedy Tickets can drop have also been expanded. 

## Rank Up Trials

Rank up trials are being slightly redone to use a bit more than just an infinite vessel fight. Rank up trials are being replaced as the primary source of greed coins, and instead are meant to be a bit of power gating for people to refine their builds and skills. Intra rank tests, like from Hunter 1 to Hunter 2, will be vessel fights where the vessel has a set HP value that must be depleted, and a starting damage/speed multiplier that ramps up over the fight to ensure players finish somewhat quickly. Rank jump tests, such as from scavenger to looter, will be Hyper objective vaults that are explicitly toned down from the real thing. They will get progressively harder as ranks increase, and are designed to be a test of a player’s overall skillset and power to prove that they really have the vault hunting skill it takes to be considered a higher tier hunter. 

These trials will still provide larger, fixed amounts of greed coins, increasing per level. However, the main source of greed coins is shifting to completion crates and assassins with the new Greed Medallion system. 

## Greed Medallions

To replace the pesky system of greed tiers making vaults harder than players want them to be without providing any real additional reward, they are being replaced with Greed Medallions, a new item that can be added to a crystal in the crystal workbench alongside everything else to infuse a crystal with additional rewards and difficulties. They are crafted by the player in a new table, the Medallion Infuser, and function in tiers identical to the rank system – you can make a Scavenger 3 Medallion, or a Champion 1 Medallion, and so on, and apply them to crystals. You can craft any medallion up to and including your personal rank, so as a Vault Looter 2 you could craft Scav 1-3 and Looter 1-2, but not Looter 3+. A crystal can only have a single Medallion applied to it, and is locked from having more applied to them after the first. Additionally, a player can only apply a Medallion that is up to and including their rank, to try and limit the amount of Medallion selling to newer players from higher rank ones, though you can still enter any vault no matter your rank or the vault crystal’s rank once it is crafted/placed. Note that Medallion buffs are multiplicative with all other modifiers, for things like crate loot or mob stats. # God Alignments

To address the abomination that is the greed skill tree, it is being removed and replaced with a pseudo class system called the God Alignment system, where players gain reputation with each of the four god factions to spec into specialized ability trees themed around each god. Reputation is gained with each god separately, and to level up standing with each god you must perform two tasks: gain enough God XP, from completing altars and mapped vaults of that God’s type (more on that in a second), and by sacrificing items to the god in the repurposed greed cauldron. By completing the necessary sacrifices and altars/vaults, one can tier up with a specific god to unlock more of their skill tree and powers.

Since there are 4 gods, there will be 4 separate god trees, each composed of 4 kinds of nodes: *Basic Nodes*, which provide run of the mill basic stats, *Minor Nodes* which provide rarer stats and some functionality changes, and *Major Nodes* which give large changes and bonuses to the player. You can only have one active god tree at a time, and all 3 types of nodes on that tree will be active, but other trees can still impact the player somewhat – 25% of stats from basic nodes will carry over from other trees, and a few select minor nodes of the player’s choice will carry over as well. Major nodes are locked specifically to that active tree, and multiple major nodes from different gods can never be used. Some minor nodes that alter functionality of the same feature might be mutually exclusive, but in general as much compatibility as possible is allowed for fun combinations. The 4 trees are each themed around their god type: Idona is responsible for brute force attack damage and ability damage, Velara has support and survivability nodes, Tenos has loot focused nodes, and Wendarr has utility and vault time nodes. 

Mapped Vaults have been changed slightly to integrate nicely into the God Alignment system. Maps can now be thought of as ‘God quests’, that are objectives issued by specific gods to complete. By default, all maps now drop with an unchangeable god type on them, that is the vault god they will grant god xp for. Maps also roll with a new “Bonus XP” implicit, that will drop significantly if modified in the artisan table in any way to encourage people to enjoy the map’s natural objective instead of target farming whatever is inevitably fastest. Different objectives will give different amounts of XP, and altars in mapped vaults will give extra XP as well.

### God Ultimates (5 minute CD, can’t be altered)

These are new ultimate abilities given by unlocking their respective god tree nodes. They exist as a new ability in the ultimate tab, and are all grouped under one ability, which by default displays as “Stirrings of Power”, that is a placeholder ability that does nothing. Once a suitable god tree with an unlocked ultimate is selected, the ability will automatically change into the respective god ultimate. If the god tree is changed, the ability will automatically change to reflect the correct ultimate for the tree, or go back to the placeholder if the tree doesn’t have its ultimate unlocked yet. The full configs for each ability are available on the spreadsheet. 

Copé de Grâce (Idona): Infuse yourself with the power of Idona, gaining superhuman damage, ability power, and resistance for a short time. At the end of the infusion, a powerful dash forward is activated, dealing damage in a radius to everything you pass through based on the cumulative attack and ability damage you dealt during the infusion. 

Savior (Velara): Infuse yourself with the power of Velara, sending a shockwave across the entire vault that heals and shields all players in the vault for a large amount, gives them resistance, and can even revive downed teammates (only works once per person). 

Eyes of God (Tenos): Infuse yourself with the power of Tenos, and instantly discover surrounding rooms far away from you.

Bullet Time (Wendarr): Infuse yourself with the power of Wendarr, temporarily slowing down the vault timer, gaining bonus movement and attack speed, and superhuman reflexes allowing you to dodge almost any attack for a short time. 

### God Charms

To go along with the god tree rework, god charms will also get a makeover. Most importantly, the god charm you are using will determine the currently active god tree, so you’ll select which god tree you’re using via what charm you have equipped. God charms have been changed to scale off of god specific Piety instead of reputation, so that multiple sources can improve them, though the direct scaling remains the same: 1 reputation \= 10 piety, and stats scale the way they did before for every 10 piety. God level, god reputation, and sacrifices can all increase your Piety, which will all contribute to the power of God Charms. Additionally, mythic versions of god charms are added, which have more things to roll from, wider roll ranges, and the potential for legendary rolls, making them more versatile.
